I have taught mathematics at undergraduate and master's levels at the University of Oxford, and have mentored students through research and outreach programmes in the UK and Germany.

## Teaching at Oxford

**Mathematics tutor (2020–2023)**

- **Part C (fourth-year undergraduate / master's):** Algebraic Topology (including a visiting student).
- **Part B (third-year undergraduate):** Commutative Algebra; Algebraic Number Theory.
- **Part A (second-year undergraduate):** Rings and Modules; Number Theory (including a visiting student).

**Teaching assistant (2018–2020)**

- **Part C:** Homological Algebra; Algebraic Topology; Differential Manifolds.
- **Part B:** Galois Theory; Introduction to Representation Theory; Commutative Algebra; Algebraic Number Theory.
- **Part A:** Linear Algebra; Rings and Modules.

## Research Supervision and Mentoring

- **Max Planck Institute for Mathematics, Bonn (2026):** Supervised five undergraduate students in a summer research experience project on single-valued multiple zeta values and the *f*-alphabet.
- **University of Oxford (2024):** Mentored a Master's student through the [Mathematical Institute's Online Research Projects with Africa (MIORPA)](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/outreach/miorpa) programme.
- **University of Oxford (2020–2021):** Academic mentor and tutor for an undergraduate student with special educational needs (SEN) in Mathematics and Philosophy.
- **University of Oxford:** Supervised three doctoral students undertaking training to become mathematics tutors.

## Outreach and Enrichment

- **Debate Chamber summer school (2026):** Mathematics tutor for secondary-school students in the UK.
- **Oxford UNIQ summer school (2021, 2022):** Number Theory tutor for prospective Oxford applicants from UK state schools.
- **Oxford Mathematics Admissions Test workshop (2022):** Led an admissions preparation workshop for Year 12 students at Balliol College, Oxford.
- **Oxford Maths Festival (2019, 2020):** Volunteer supporting public mathematical outreach activities.
