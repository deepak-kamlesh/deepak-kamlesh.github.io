## About Me

I am a mathematician working at the interface of **arithmetic geometry, number theory and mathematical physics**, with particular interests in periods, motives, special functions and scattering amplitudes.

I obtained my DPhil in Mathematics from the **University of Oxford** in 2024, under the supervision of **Francis Brown**. I subsequently held a postdoctoral fellowship at the **Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in Bonn**.

My doctoral research investigated motivic coactions on generalised hypergeometric functions and their applications to the structure of string amplitudes. In joint work, we introduced **zeta generators** and used them to reformulate motivic coactions and single-valued maps for multiple zeta values and multiple polylogarithms. A key feature of the resulting framework is its independence from the genus of the underlying geometry, making it applicable, in principle, to iterated integrals arising in different geometric settings. This framework has since inspired further developments at genus one, particularly in the study of iterated integrals arising in string amplitudes.
