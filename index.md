I am a mathematician working at the interface of **arithmetic geometry, number theory and mathematical physics**, with particular interests in periods, motives, special functions and scattering amplitudes.

I obtained my DPhil in Mathematics from the **[University of Oxford](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/)** in 2024, under the supervision of **[Francis Brown](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/people/francis.brown)**. I subsequently held a postdoctoral fellowship at the **[Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in Bonn](https://www.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/)**.

My doctoral research investigated motivic coactions on generalised hypergeometric functions and their applications to the structure of string amplitudes. In joint work, we introduced **[zeta generators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00697)** and used them to reformulate motivic coactions and single-valued maps for multiple zeta values and multiple polylogarithms. A key feature of the resulting framework is its independence from the genus of the underlying geometry, making it applicable, in principle, to iterated integrals arising in different geometric settings. This framework has since inspired further developments at genus one, particularly in the study of iterated integrals arising in string amplitudes.

## Research Interests

My research is broadly concerned with the arithmetic and geometric structures underlying periods and special functions, and their connections to quantum field theory and string theory. I am particularly interested in the mathematical structures emerging at the interfaces of these areas.

My research interests include:

- **Periods, motives and arithmetic geometry:** Algebraic relations among periods, motivic Galois theory and coactions, p-adic periods and q-deformations, Mahler measures and regulators.

- **Special functions, iterated integrals and fundamental groups:** Multiple zeta values, polylogarithms, generalised hypergeometric functions, Drinfeld associators and Grothendieck–Teichmüller theory.

- **Motivic structures at higher genus:** Zeta generators, motivic coactions and single-valued maps for higher-genus iterated integrals, with connections to scattering amplitudes.

- **Scattering amplitudes, geometry and integrability:** Feynman integrals, string amplitudes, positive geometry, quantum integrability and the mathematical structures of quantum field theory.

- **Exponential periods and non-perturbative structures:** Exponential motives, irregular differential equations, Stokes phenomena and resurgence, with applications to exact and non-perturbative amplitudes.
